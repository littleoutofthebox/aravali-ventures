01 · About the summit

An evening for the people who build, and the people who back them.

Our cities have no shortage of business experience or capital. What investors often lack is a reliable way to find and judge the right early-stage opportunity. The summit brings a curated group of investors and founders into one room, with startups screened and profiled in advance, so every conversation starts with clarity instead of a sales pitch.

05 · Why Udaipur

The Aravalis are among the oldest mountains on earth. Good ideas deserve the same staying power.

Udaipur is a city of lakes and palaces, ringed by ancient hills, and it has long been a place where serious agreements are made. It is also where Aravali Ventures begins: in the heart of the business communities we want to connect with founders across India.
